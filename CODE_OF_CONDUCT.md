# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project respectful, constructive, and harassment-free for everyone.

## Expected Behavior

- Be clear, specific, and evidence-oriented.
- Critique ideas and artifacts, not people.
- Assume good faith while preserving technical rigor.
- Respect privacy and do not post sensitive, proprietary, or personal data.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, or discriminatory language.
- Personal attacks or repeated bad-faith disruption.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Submitting secrets, exploit payloads, or customer data as public examples.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or restrict participation when behavior conflicts with this code of conduct.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant v2.1 intent and kept concise for the draft repository.
